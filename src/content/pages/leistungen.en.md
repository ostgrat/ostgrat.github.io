  - question: Who is Ostgrat the right partner for?
    answer: For mid-sized companies with roughly 50 to 2,000 employees that need to protect their IT and often their production too – too big for off-the-shelf solutions, too small for their own security team. Most of our clients are based in Austria and the DACH region.
  - question: How quickly do you respond in an emergency?
    answer: SOC clients can reach us around the clock; we respond in under 15 minutes. With an incident response retainer, the response time is contractually guaranteed. The emergency hotline is also available to companies that are not yet clients.
  - question: Does NIS2 apply to us?
    answer: That depends mainly on your sector, company size and your role in the supply chain. In an applicability check we clarify this within one day and show you exactly which obligations you will face.
  - question: Will a pentest disrupt our operations?
    answer: No, not if it's well planned. We agree on test windows and limits with you in advance, test production systems only passively or during maintenance windows, and always have a direct emergency contact during the test.
  - question: Where does our data stay?
    answer: In Austria. Our SOC is in Linz; we process log data and reports in our own systems in line with the GDPR and never pass them on.
  - question: What does it cost to get started?
    answer: A pentest starts at € 6,900, the managed SOC at € 2,400 per month. The first conversation is free – afterwards you receive a quote with a fixed scope.
